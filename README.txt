OUR SPACE — GROUPED AT A GLANCE (5 KPIs)

WHAT CHANGED
- At a glance stays collapsed on load and below Your Inbox.
- When expanded: Our Next Stop (Places to visit, Places we've been), Our Places To (On our list, Ticked off), Our Music (Tracks shared).
- Music total counts saved entries in musicTracks, including current jams and older songs, exactly once per shared entry. It refreshes when the live music listener updates (share/delete included).
- KPI cards continue to open the associated section.
- Based on the latest Music-polish website files, plus the subsequent Music profile-avatar changes.

INSTALL
1. Back up the currently working website in GitHub.
2. Replace index.html, app.js and styles.css in the GitHub Pages repository root with these three files.
3. Commit, then refresh the page (hard refresh if you see old styles).
4. Open At a glance and verify the 3 groups, 5 numbers, and navigation. Share or delete a Music track and verify Tracks shared changes.

FIREBASE
No Firestore rule or account changes needed. No changes to existing data.

TESTING
HTML DOM structure and JavaScript syntax checked locally. Not tested against live Firebase or Spotify.
